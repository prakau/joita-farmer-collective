JOITA BIOSEED AI - SOIL SAATHI 4.2.0

ANDROID INSTALLATION

1. Download or copy JOITA-BioSeed-AI-Soil-Saathi-4.2.0.apk to the Android phone.
2. Open the file from Files, WhatsApp, email or another trusted transfer app.
3. If Android asks, allow that transfer app to install unknown apps.
4. Tap Install (or Update when a JOITA 4.x version is already installed), then open “JOITA BioSeed AI – Soil Saathi”.
5. Connect the soil sensor with USB OTG. The app opens directly to the eight measurements and asks for USB access automatically.
6. Fully insert the probes into moist soil at root depth. Keep them still; wait 3–5 seconds for most values and longer for pH when needed.
7. Read the Low / In range / High labels and the farmer actions below the values. Test 3–5 fresh nearby spots before a field decision.
8. Add farmer, father, village or mobile details only if you want them printed on the report. They are optional and never block the reading.
9. Tap Save to keep the reading, Download PDF to save the structured report, or Share to send it.
10. Use EN/हिन्दी at the top to change language and the book icon to open the full measurement guide.

The app needs Android 6.0 or newer. USB OTG is optional; manual entry still works when the sensor is unavailable.

This JOITA app installs alongside the old SoilDetector app. It updates earlier JOITA 4.x releases normally and keeps their saved data.
