# Register farmers on your Android phone

Install the latest Joita Farmer Collective APK. Tap **Update** if version 1.0 is already installed; do not uninstall it. The app works offline after installation.

## 1. Register a farmer

Tap **Add farmer**. Enter name, phone, village, land arrangement (owned/tenant/shared), lead-farmer status, and family/farming notes. Add a farmer photo with permission, enter the registering officer and add GPS coordinates if available. Read back the information. Record acknowledgement only if the farmer agrees. Tap **SAVE**.

The phone assigns a registration reference such as JOITA-1. References are local to each phone and can repeat on other phones. Acknowledgement records the officer's statement and phone time, not a digital signature or binding agreement.

## 2. Add their fields

Open the farmer and tap **Add field**. Record acreage, crop, variety, season, sowing date, soil type, irrigation, inputs used, and plot/location/boundary notes. Note the official plot reference and ownership/tenancy details where known. Acreage and coordinates are entered observations, not surveyed boundaries.

## 3. Record each field visit

Record date, officer, crop stage, observations, pest/disease issues, recommendations, harvest/yield with units, and a photo. In **Climate activity and evidence notes**, record:

- Activity and actual date (for example, biochar application, planting, or another program activity).
- Material quantity and unit, source/supplier and batch reference where relevant.
- Application method, affected area/plot, and baseline or before/after observations.
- Witness name, monitoring measurements, and references to receipts or laboratory reports.

The current app supports one registration photo and one photo per visit. Record separate visits for additional dated evidence, clearly describing their purpose. Keep signed program agreements, receipts, laboratory certificates and any required boundary surveys separately; this app does not collect every possible verification document.

## 4. Save a copy outside the app

Open **Reports**, choose a farmer, then **Save PDF to phone** or **Save evidence folder (.zip)**. Choose Downloads or a connected storage provider. The PDF contains the profile, fields, visits and photo appendix. The ZIP contains the PDF, structured records, available originals, image source metadata and checksums.

Send an agreed copy to the farmer and retain another securely away from the phone. Photos and entered information can support review but are not independent proof of consent, land ownership, carbon outcomes or certification. Confirm the actual Climate Collective program's checklist and retention requirements. No cloud sync or ZIP restore is provided in this release; uninstalling/clearing the app removes local records.
