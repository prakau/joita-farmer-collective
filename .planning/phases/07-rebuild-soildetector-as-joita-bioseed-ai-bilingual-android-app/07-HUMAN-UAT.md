# Phase 7 Human UAT

Use the signed APK in `joita-biosoil-android/release/` on at least one Android 6–10 phone and one Android 13–16 phone. Record phone brand/model, Android version, sensor model and OTG cable.

- [ ] Install the APK, launch it and confirm the JOITA launcher name/icon.
- [ ] Confirm the exact JOITA logo is not cropped or stretched on first run and About.
- [ ] Choose English, add a farmer/field, save a manual test, reopen it from history and share the PDF offline.
- [ ] Switch to Hindi and confirm Home, Fields, source chooser, reading, result, guidance and report actions remain readable at large system text size.
- [ ] Deny then grant location; confirm recovery and captured coordinates/accuracy.
- [ ] Open the phone camera, capture a soil photo and confirm the review evidence state.
- [ ] On a non-OTG phone, confirm manual/sample paths remain available and USB shows an understandable recovery message.
- [ ] On an OTG phone, connect the exact JOITA probe, accept USB access and confirm all eight values update only after a complete response.
- [ ] Unplug/reconnect the probe during reading and confirm retry/recovery without invented sample values.
- [ ] Confirm every sample record and report remains visibly labelled Sample.
- [ ] Confirm the advisory disclaimer appears on result and PDF report.
- [ ] Install a higher-version test build signed with the same JOITA key and confirm Android accepts it as an update without deleting local records.
