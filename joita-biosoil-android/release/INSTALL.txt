JOITA FARMER COLLECTIVE 1.1.0

ANDROID INSTALLATION

1. Scan JOITA-Farmer-Collective-INSTALL-QR.svg with the Android camera.
2. Open the latest GitHub release and tap JOITA-Farmer-Collective-1.1.0.apk.
3. Approve the download warning if Android displays one.
4. Open the downloaded APK. If asked, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
5. Tap Install, then open Joita Farmer Collective.

The app supports Android 6.0 and newer. Farmer, field, visit and photo data remains on the phone. Open Reports to save a complete farmer record and its photos as a PDF in Downloads, Drive, or another chosen location. Keep a separate backup of important PDFs and phone data.
