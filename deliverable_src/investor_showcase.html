Presentation rule

Do not narrate Joita as "software for carbon." Narrate it as a proof-and-payout engine for field operations. Investors will remember the trust chain and the farmer outcome far more than another climate-tech category slide.

India-first execution

The local strategy is part of the product, not a footnote.

Joita should present India-specific readiness as thoughtful architecture: payout rails, data boundaries, and future government-system adapters that do not overcomplicate the MVP.

Farmer payout rails

Use PSP-backed UPI and bank workflows so the prototype can move cash credibly without deep banking integration overhead.

India-resident data boundaries

Keep PII, payout records, and consent artifacts separated from MRV evidence and treated as trust controls from day one.

Future-ready government hooks

Show adapter points for Farmer Registry or parcel references as roadmap strength, while keeping the MVP sharply scoped.
