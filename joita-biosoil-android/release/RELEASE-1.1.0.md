# Joita Farmer Collective 1.1.0

Download **JOITA-Farmer-Collective-1.1.0.apk** below on your Android phone. Open it and tap **Update** (or **Install** for a first installation). Do not uninstall the old version: updating keeps your farmer records.

## Register and document farmers

- Refreshed dashboard, farmer register, field journal and reports.
- Farmer photos directly on registration; officer name and optional acknowledgement recorded with device time.
- Edit farmer, field and visit records while retaining linked history.
- Camera/gallery photos with original received images retained, resized previews and file hashes.
- Search farmers by crop/contact/village, filter lead farmers, and review visits in calendar order.
- Capture a recent GPS fix, open locations in Maps, and dial farmer contacts.
- Save full farmer PDFs with photos directly to the phone, or share them.
- Export an evidence ZIP with structured records, PDF, available original photos and checksums.

**Start:** Add farmer → add photo and officer → save → add field → record visits. For export: Reports → select farmer → Save PDF or Save evidence folder.

Records remain offline on the phone. Export copies before changing phones. Acknowledgement is the officer's record, not an electronic signature. Confirm the relevant program's evidence requirements; this app does not independently verify carbon claims or guarantee acceptance by Climate Collective.

Android 6.0+. Signed with the existing JOITA release certificate; updates version 1.0.0 in place.
