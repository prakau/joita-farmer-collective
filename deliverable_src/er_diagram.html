Project Joita-V2

Distributed Architecture & Spatial Schema

India-scale MRV foundation for offline capture, fraud resistance, and zero double-counting under Article 6.2.

Edge To Registry Flow
1

Field Agent Device

React Native app captures the perimeter walk, biochar delivery log, and live sapling proof while offline.

2

WatermelonDB Edge Store

SQLite stores append-only events, evidence manifests, and sync checkpoints until a stable 4G window appears.

3

Sync Gateway

Idempotency keys, device auth, and replay protection prevent duplicate polygon uploads during unstable connectivity.

4

PostGIS + Event Store

GeoJSON, geometry, H3 cells, and cryptographically chained events become the source of truth.

5

MRV Rules Engine

Geofence checks, overlap scans, EXIF validation, and duplicate detection produce auditable verification results.

6

Issuance & Registry

Verified removals are aggregated into issuance lots and linked to sovereign registry records for ITMO issuance.

Core Relationships
Relation Purpose
farmers 1:N farm_polygons Each farmer can hold multiple mapped plots across fragmented landholdings.
farm_polygons 1:N biochar_ledger Every biochar application is anchored to a verified polygon and a geofenced capture point.
farm_polygons 1:N tree_inventory Tree records remain parcel-specific for future survival, canopy, and sequestration checks.
farm_polygons 1:N polygon_h3_cells H3 cells accelerate overlap detection and portfolio-level heatmaps without expensive polygon scans.
evidence_assets linked to ledger + trees Photos, signatures, and EXIF proofs are re-usable evidence objects instead of opaque blobs in business tables.
Append-Only Event Store

event_store

  • PK event_store_id uuid
  • aggregate_type text
  • aggregate_id uuid
  • event_type text
  • payload jsonb
  • prev_hash text
  • curr_hash text
  • device_signature text
  • recorded_at timestamptz
Tamper-proof chain: curr_hash = SHA-256(payload || recorded_at || prev_hash). Any change to a past event breaks the hash chain and becomes immediately visible to auditors.

All farmer, polygon, biochar, tree, and payout events are written here first and projected into read models for dashboards and registry exports.

Aadhaar data is not stored in Joita databases. The platform keeps only an adv_reference_token issued by a certified Aadhaar Data Vault, which keeps the design aligned with India's DPDP posture.
